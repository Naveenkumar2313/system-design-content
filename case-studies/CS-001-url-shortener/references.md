## Evidence policy for interns

For any claim about a real vendor's actual implementation:
- Prefer official engineering blogs, technical documentation, conference talks, or published papers.
- Record title, publisher, URL, publication date, access date, and the exact claim supported.
- Label third-party analysis as secondary evidence.
- Do not convert a common design pattern into a claim about Bitly's internal architecture.
